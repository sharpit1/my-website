Real-World Safety · Adversarial Robustness · Visual Privacy

Hello! I am Dongsu Song. Thank you for your interest. I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Korea Aerospace University, advised by Prof. Jay Hoon Jung, and I am seeking a Ph.D. position abroad. I received my M.S. degree in Artificial Intelligence from the same university in 2025 under his supervision.

My research focuses on the reliability of vision models: finding where they fail, understanding why, and making them more robust. I began with black-box sparse attacks that expose a model’s weaknesses by perturbing only a few pixels under limited query access (NeurIPS 2024, ECCV 2026). I have since moved from pixel-level noise to natural semantic changes, such as a shift in lighting to dusk, that degrade models across architectures. I also repurpose adversarial perturbations to protect images and videos against unauthorized generative editing and image animation. Going forward, I want to extend this work to video, multimodal foundation models, and vision-language-action (VLA) models, and study how visual failures propagate from perception to action.

Research Interests

  • Real-World Safety: Using generative models to uncover realistic semantic shifts that break vision models, diagnosing why they fail, and turning these insights into more robust models
  • Adversarial Robustness: Finding and understanding adversarial vulnerabilities of vision systems, building on black-box sparse attacks and extending to video, multimodal foundation models, and vision-language-action (VLA) models
  • Visual Privacy: Repurposing adversarial perturbations to protect images and videos against unauthorized generative editing and image animation
